# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We commit to making participation in this project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity or expression, experience, education, socioeconomic status, nationality, appearance, race, caste, color, religion, sexual identity or orientation, or technical choices.

We will act and interact in ways that support an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Expected behavior

Examples of behavior that helps the community include:

- showing empathy and respect;
- giving and accepting constructive feedback;
- focusing disagreement on ideas and evidence;
- accepting responsibility, apologizing, and learning from mistakes;
- protecting other people's privacy and security.

## Unacceptable behavior

Unacceptable behavior includes:

- sexualized language or attention, unwanted advances, or harassment;
- trolling, insults, threats, or sustained disruption;
- public or private harassment;
- publishing another person's private information without permission;
- exposing credentials, session content, or security details that put others at risk;
- other conduct that would reasonably be inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Scope

This Code applies in repository spaces and when a person officially represents the project in public spaces. Project spaces include issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions, and other project-managed communication.

## Reporting and enforcement

Report conduct problems privately to the repository maintainers. Use a private contact method shown on the repository owner's GitHub profile. If none is available, open an issue requesting private contact without including the incident details.

Maintainers will review reports as promptly and fairly as practical. They may remove or edit content and may warn, temporarily restrict, or permanently exclude participants whose behavior they judge inappropriate, threatening, or harmful. Maintainers will respect the privacy and safety of reporters as far as practical, but cannot promise absolute confidentiality or a specific outcome.

Project maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code in good faith may be removed from the project team by the repository owner.
